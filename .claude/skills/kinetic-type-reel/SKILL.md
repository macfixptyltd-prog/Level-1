# Kinetic Type Reel

Turn a clip of someone talking into a reel in one of the reference styles the user loves. **Style A** (a Florida real-estate ad): huge stacked words that sit **behind the speaker's head**, a second stack **on the chest**, key words that are **thrown in** with motion blur, a **"negative" blend** that lets the footage show through the coloured words, strike-throughs, whip cuts, zooms, image cards, B-roll and **sound effects on every move**. **Style B** (an event promo, Global Games Show): one huge glossy word at a time behind the head with a small italic kicker word sitting on it, numbers that rise out of a slot with "th" superscripts, rounded photo/video cards popping in around the speaker, 3D icons bursting out of one point, small italic labels on the chest, a B-roll montage with one centred italic phrase per beat, and a black end card with a green glow and an outlined pill. **Style C** (a LinkedIn podcast clip): calm lowercase type: small words spaced wide on one line, then one big hero word under them that ghosts in, a tail line hanging under it, a yellow handwritten accent word, hand-drawn arrows, B-roll with a word-by-word lower-third bar, rack-focus transitions and a white "about me" page. **Style D** (a bright-studio service promo): see 1d. **Style E** (a 16:9 LinkedIn interview clip, host + guest): sentence captions that build word by word on the empty side of the frame, one hero word per phrase in a different, heavier or condensed font and a meaning colour (pink for the threat, lime for the human answer, amber for the payoff), a letter-by-letter typed name card over a cut-out photo and book, a "decode" reveal where the initials of a phrase land first, AI-generated still B-roll with slow pushes and a hard whip blur into it, and an open-loop ending that cuts mid-sentence. Pick the style that fits the client (or mix them); ask only if the user has not said. Reply to the user in Persian; on-screen text follows the language of the speech.

Nothing here is a template. Fonts, colours, which words are big, where the stacks sit and how they move are decided again for each video from what the speaker says and what the footage looks like. The reference sets the grammar and the quality bar only.

## 1. Style A grammar (measured frame by frame)

- **Two stacks, timed separately.** TOP stack: 2-3 tight lines above and behind the head; the head hides the middle of the lowest word, which gives depth. CHEST stack: 1-3 lines over the chest that finish the sentence ("You know that agent" up top, "in your market" on the chest). A chest stack often stays while the next top stack arrives. Connectives ("and", "because", "to be") appear alone on the chest.
- **Three sizes in one family.** key = heaviest weight, about 2.6x the small size, coloured. mid = bold, about 1.5x, white or grey. small = light or regular, white. Tight tracking, lines almost touching.
- **Words land on the spoken beat.** Most pop in; one or two per sentence are thrown in from the side in about 0.2 s with a long motion-blur streak; some get a light sweep after landing.
- **"Negative" key words** (the user says "navigate"): the coloured word is bright over dark footage and muted over bright footage, with the texture of the footage visible inside the letters. Red words stay solid.
- A cut or framing change every 5-6 s, a whip blur on the big scene change, image cards at the moment the thing is named (incoming call, follower list), a strike-through on a claim being denied.

## 1b. Style B grammar (measured frame by frame)

- **One hero word per beat, behind the head.** Big grotesque sans (Inter / SF Pro feel), semibold, very tight tracking, about 23% of the frame width tall in a wide shot. Fill is a vertical **gradient**: silver (white -> light grey -> mid grey) for most words, lime green (#DBFF73 -> #86E53C -> #41B82B) for places, numbers, the brand and the payoff. A soft drop shadow. The head covers the middle of the word.
- **Kicker words attached to the hero.** The small connective words are not a separate line: "The" sits on the top-left corner of "Global", "Game" sits under it, "Crowne" above "Plaza", "on" above "June", "Join us" above "Connect", "from around the" above "World". Italic of the same family, medium weight, about a quarter of the hero size, white, always in front of the speaker.
- **Numbers rise out of a slot** at their own baseline (masked, about 0.3 s) and get small superscript ordinals ("th") a beat later. Dates and amounts go green.
- **Media cards.** While the speaker lists things ("game developers, industry leaders..."), 3-5 rounded cards (radius about 10% of the short side) with photos or video loops pop in one after another (about 0.15 s apart) around the speaker, some behind her and some in front, drifting slowly upward; the hero word sits on top of them.
- **Icon burst.** On a category word ("gaming", "esports") glossy 3D icons pop out of one point on the chest, fly to a ring with overshoot, spin a little and pop away.
- **Labels on the chest.** Small italic phrases ("Bringing together", "shaping the future of", "Interactive") that carry the sentence between hero words.
- **Montage close.** In the B-roll section each phrase is a centred white italic caption, one or two lines, cut on the beat. Then a black end card: logo, outlined pill ("JOIN US"), date and venue in small sans, a green glow at the bottom.
- Persistent small logo watermark at the top centre. Quick cuts; a flash or whip on scene changes.

## 1c. Style C grammar (measured frame by frame: a 16:9 LinkedIn podcast clip)

A calm, documentary podcast edit. Everything is **lowercase**, one clean grotesque (SF Pro / Inter feel), **white only**, plus one yellow accent (#FFE600). It works in 16:9 and in 9:16.

- **Spaced small words, then one hero word.** The phrase builds word by word on one small line with **very wide gaps** (about 0.8-1.0 of the font size between words: "i   felt   like   i   was"). Small words just appear on their syllable, with no motion. Then the hero word lands **directly under the line, about 3.5-4x bigger**, tight tracking, with almost no leading, so the small line sits on the hero's cap height. Both are left-aligned on the empty side of the frame, in the upper third.
- **Hero word "ghost-in".** It appears at about 45% opacity (grey over the dark set) for 2 frames, then goes solid white. No scale bounce, no throw. This one detail is the signature of the style.
- **Tail line.** The rest of the sentence hangs under the hero in small type, left-aligned to it ("long" -> "period of time", "depends" -> "on the actions of others", "job" -> "tomorrow"), filling in word by word.
- **Behind the head.** When the hero word crosses the speaker, it goes behind the head ("five years", "happiness", "was").
- **Yellow handwritten accent.** Sometimes a short brush-script word in yellow sits under or beside the hero, tilted about -6 degrees and written on ("saying" + *yes*, "gone" + *any further*).
- **Hand-drawn annotations on B-roll.** A thin marker word plus an arrow that draws itself toward the person ("i say ←").
- **B-roll captions switch to a lower third.** Over cinematic B-roll the big type stops. A small white sentence builds word by word inside a fixed, translucent dark bar at the bottom left. The bar is already sized for the whole sentence, so the words fill it in.
- **B-roll grade.** Fashion and archive B-roll is often black-and-white or crushed, with cuts on the beat. B-roll runs 2-6 s, then back to the speaker.
- **Transitions.** A rack-focus blur on the speaker (0.3-0.5 s) into the B-roll, a quick cross-blur, or a hard cut. Text stays sharp while the picture blurs. There are no whips.
- **"About me" page.** On a self-referential beat ("about me", "about all of it"), the key word gets a yellow highlight box that wipes on. The frame then flashes to a white page, and the next B-roll plays in a plain rectangular window that grows from small to large, with black text above it.
- **Reaction cutaways** to the host (if the source has a second camera) on questions. One phrase per stack, and the whole stack is replaced by a hard cut when the next phrase starts. Soft sound only: a pop on hero words, air on the blur transitions, a flick on handwriting.

## 1d. Style D grammar (measured frame by frame: a 22 s 9:16 real-estate video-editor promo, 24 fps)

A clean, bright "agency service" promo. One continuous shot of a seated speaker on a seamless light-grey studio background (#E8ECF0). There are almost no hard cuts: every change comes from overlays, reframes and object transitions. Text is dark on light.

- **Glow-in hero word (signature).** The hero word is first a soft neon bloom of its own colour (a blurred halo about 2x the word, about 60% opacity) with the letters blurred inside it; over about 0.3 s the blur collapses and the word lands sharp while the halo fades. Used on: selling, matters, together. Measured: glow at 0.15 s, sharp at 0.5 s.
- **Hero + kicker.** The hero is a tight, heavy grotesque, about 70% of the frame width, set above or behind the head (the head cuts into it). A small dark kicker in a regular weight sits on its top-left or top-right shoulder ("you're" + selling, "why do your" + videos + "look like" under it, "that capture" + attention, "if you're a real estate" + agent, "let's work" + together). Kickers build word by word before the hero.
- **Gradient fills per meaning.** Green gradient (#038E00 -> #0AA600 -> #1AB014) for the promise and positive words; a deep red gradient (about #C0101A -> #7A0A10) for the pain word ("videos" that look bad); an Instagram gradient (magenta #E1306C -> orange #F7A21B -> purple #833AB4) on the word "social". Everything else is near-black.
- **Grey-to-black karaoke lines.** Side sentences ("and make your listings stand out", "ready to elevate your social media") are laid out in full in light grey (about #BFC4CA) and each word turns near-black as it is spoken. Left-aligned, small, tight leading, placed next to the speaker or a card.
- **Exit = blur + fade.** Words and cards leave by blurring and fading over about 0.25 s, never by throwing.
- **Decor ticks.** Tiny outlined rounded squares (about 2% of the width) float on either side of the first hero word and drift outward.
- **Photo fan / reel collage.** On the hook, 3-4 property photos fan out from behind the hero word on one side; on "why do your videos look like...", 10-12 small reel-thumbnail cards burst around the speaker in a loose ring, front and behind, then blur out.
- **Device-screen transition (the big move).** The speaker scales down to about 45% and slides left; a cut-out camera on a tripod slides in from the right with overshoot and its LCD plays the live speaker (screen inside screen), under a hero word ("raw property footage"). Then a hand holding a phone slides in from the left, pushes the camera out, and the phone screen replays the edited opening ("you're selling" with the photo fan) = before/after in one move. The phone holds about 1 s, then a hard cut back to the full speaker. Total about 2.6 s (4.4-7.0 s).
- **Reframe for a side card.** On "make your listings stand out" a tall rounded video card (about 40% x 50% of the frame, playing a slow push on a house) grows in on the left while the speaker pans right to make room; the karaoke line sits top right; then the card blurs out and the speaker recentres with a slight push-in.
- **UI chips.** Services appear as white pill chips with a small line icon and soft shadow ("Cinematic Transitions", "Professional SFX", "Motion Graphics", "Storytelling"), popping in one by one about 0.5 s apart into a 2x2 grid over the chest, with a small scale overshoot.
- **Profile pill and social card.** A small pill with an avatar, name and role ("Emma Wilson / Real estate agent") on the chest; later an Instagram-style profile card (avatar ring, verified tick, posts / followers / following) whose numbers count up (108 -> 384, 22K -> 57K) while the hero word sits above.
- **Typing UI bars (CTA).** A white search bar with a purple filter button types "Real Est...", then morphs into a message composer: a small chat line "Let's create something exceptional" types above it, a purple tag pill ("Real Estate") pops on the right, mic and image icons, blinking caret. Purple UI accent about #6C2BD9.
- **Sound.** A soft music bed under the voice the whole time (floor about -33 dB) and a short SFX on every visual event: glow-in shimmer, card whooshes, the camera slide, the phone slide, the cut back, chip pops, keyboard clicks on the typing bars.

**Building Style D with this engine.** Hero + kicker: `fill` gradients with `attach`. Cards, fan, collage: `inserts` with `grow`, `drift`, `layer`. Side card: an `inserts` video plus a `zooms` item whose `focus` moves right. Built into kt.py (first used on a realtor reel, Nov 2026): word `"in": "glow"` with `"glow": "#33E07C"` (halo colour), block `"karaoke": "#A3A9B0"` (grey copy of every word until it is spoken; use `"in": "fade"` on that block) and block `"out": "blur"`. A two-word hero ("right buyers", "biggest asset") goes in a smaller hero role (about 200) so the whole stack does not shrink and the kickers stay readable; give top stacks `"overlap": 0.0` when a tail line hangs under the hero. Over white walls the karaoke grey must be about #A3A9B0, lighter vanishes. Done as separate passes for that job (rebuild the same way): a pre-pass on the source for the device transition (frame shrinks to a rounded card at about 0.56 on a light seamless background, a code-drawn phone slides in with overshoot and plays the first 3 s of the edit rendered beforehand, then everything reverses before the next cut; text over it uses `"at"` + front layer), white service chips as PNG `inserts` with `grow`, a listing photo card cropped from an empty part of the footage, and a post-pass for the CTA: search bar typing, then a composer with a purple sent bubble typed in sync with her words and a 1.2 s hold at the end. Music: a soft synthesized keys + pad bed mixed about 15 dB under the voice. Still to be coded for each job: the device-screen transition (cut-out camera and phone PNGs with the screen area masked, the live or edited frames composited into the screen with a perspective warp), the chips, the counting profile card and the typing bars. If a feature is built for a job, add it to kt.py as a new key and record it here.

## 1e. Style E grammar (measured frame by frame: a 43.5 s 16:9 LinkedIn interview clip, 30 fps, 1280x720)

A punchy interview cutdown: a host's questions on a podcast set (mic in frame, three-quarter profile) intercut with a guest's answers recorded on a webcam at home, plus three AI-generated stills as B-roll. Mixed-case type, white plus three meaning colours, and **a different font per role**. It reads like a "hot take" post: hook, claim, credentials, the question, the answer, a cliffhanger.

- **Structure (cut times).** 0-3.9 guest hook ("You hear this all the time that AI will replace...") -> 3.9-7.1 name card -> 7.1-9.9 AI B-roll -> 9.9-13.6 host -> 13.6-16.0 AI B-roll -> host question -> guest answer -> 26.2-28.6 AI B-roll after a whip -> host -> guest -> hard cut on "will.." at 43.4 s. One shot change about every 2.5-4 s; nothing lasts longer than about 6 s.
- **Sentence captions on the empty side.** Every word of the speech is on screen, in sync, one phrase at a time (2-7 words). Small words in a clean geometric sans (Raleway / Montserrat feel, weight 600-700), white, about 4.5% of the width tall in 16:9. Words **just appear** on their syllable: no pop, no slide, no fade. Phrases are left-aligned (or right-aligned when the empty side is on the left) next to the head, roughly at eye height, never over the face. When a phrase ends, the whole stack is cleared by a hard cut and the next phrase starts on the next word, often in a different spot.
- **Hero word = different font, about 2.5-3.5x bigger.** One word or short group per phrase, the one the sentence leans on. It changes font with the meaning: a **tall condensed heavy** face (Bebas Neue / Oswald feel) for the threat words ("AI", "brains", "JOB"), a **round black geometric** face (Montserrat 900 feel) in lowercase for the human answer ("human", "weakening", "ultimate"), a **wide bold caps** line (Montserrat 800) for the question and the method ("ACTIONABLE THINGS", "ENHANCE", "LEVERAGE AI ?"), and a **serif** (DM Serif / Merriweather feel) for the turning point ("I think the real question is", "will.."). The hero sits on the line under or beside the small words with almost no leading; a short tail line hangs under it ("to a degree", "for everything", "intelligence").
- **Meaning colours (sampled).** Pink-crimson **#C2335A** = the fear / AI / loss ("AI", "DOESN'T", "brains", "DEPEND ON AI", "JOB"). Lime **#C8E66A** = the human answer and the action ("human", "LEVERAGE AI ?"). Amber **#F0A80E** = the payoff phrase ("HUMAN INTELLIGENCE"). Everything else white. A lime word and a pink word never share a phrase.
- **Decode reveal (signature).** On the payoff phrase the letters land **out of order**: the initials first ("H I" under "ENHANCE"), then the remaining letters pop in at random, about 1.4 s for 17 letters, in the condensed font, amber. It sells "human intelligence = HI".
- **Typed name card.** On the first mention of credentials: a full-frame grey studio background (about #6E6E6E, darker vignette), a **cut-out photo** of the guest on stage on the left, a **3D book / product mockup** tilted on the right, and between them the name in heavy caps typed letter by letter (about 12 letters a second), then a two-line role in a light weight typed under it ("MEMORY EXPERT / & BESTSELLING AUTHOR"). The whole card **trucks left** at a constant speed for its full 3.2 s, so the cut-out leaves the frame and the name and book drift toward the left third. Dark navy text, not white.
- **AI-still B-roll as metaphor.** Generated images (or image-to-video clips) that illustrate the idea, not the literal words: an office of people at AI screens where the people **dissolve away one by one** ("replace"); a crowd staring at phones under a glowing **brain that morphs into an "AI" chip** ("weakening everybody's brains"); a hero on a peak raising a glowing hammer over a crowd ("I created AI" = the tool, the human holds it). Dark, cinematic, desaturated except one glow colour. Each has a slow push-in (about 4-6% over 2.5 s); the image that follows a question fades slightly toward black at its end. No captions over B-roll: the speech runs on as voice-over.
- **Transitions.** Mostly hard cuts on the phrase boundary. On the big turn ("I created AI" -> image) a **horizontal whip**: the speaker frame smears sideways over 2-3 frames, the image arrives from a smear and settles. The B-roll ends with a radial / motion blur out back to the speaker.
- **Footage treatment.** Both cameras get a soft dark vignette on the edges and a light dark gradient on the text side, so white type reads on a white wall. Slightly lifted blacks, warm skin. Both shots run a slow push-in.
- **Sound.** Voice at about -15 LUFS with a quiet music bed about 10-12 dB under it (floor about -27 dB in the pauses), with no SFX on the words. The music stops dead on the final cut, which is mid-word ("will..") to make the viewer go to the full episode.

**Building Style E with this engine.** Per role fonts: `"roles"` each with their own `"font"` (the role `font` overrides the style font), e.g. `small` Raleway 700, `cond` Bebas Neue / Oswald 700, `round` Montserrat 900, `caps` Montserrat 800, `serif` DM Serif Display. Small words `"in": "none"`; hero words `"in": "none"` too (or `"ghost"` when it needs a hint of life). Use a block per phrase with `"at"`, `"align": "left"` and `"layer": "front"`, and give it `"end"` at the next phrase so the stack clears with a hard cut. Built into kt.py (Nov 2026, for this style): word `"in": "type"` (letters appear one by one in reading direction, `"rate": 12` letters per second; a word may contain spaces, so a whole name or role line can be one word) and `"in": "decode"` (letters land in random order with the initials of each word first, `"dur": 1.4`, `"seed": 3`); both read right to left for Persian. Name card: a `solids` item (`"color": "#6E6E6E", "riser": false`), the cut-out photo and the book as `inserts` with `"drift": [-60, 0]` and `"layer": "front"`, and `type` text blocks with `"at"`. Whip: `"whips": [{"t": 26.2}]`. B-roll stills: an image-to-video clip as `broll`, or a still with a push via `zooms`; the dissolving people and the brain-to-chip morph are generated clips (ask before spending credits; Higgsfield image-to-video from a still the user approves). Vignette and text-side gradient: a pre-pass with ffmpeg `vignette=PI/4.5` plus a `geq` or overlay PNG gradient. Music bed: teaser-music-sfx skill, mixed 10-12 dB under the voice, cut dead on the last frame.

## 3. Workflow

1. **Look.** `python ~/kt/kt.py probe in.mp4 probe.jpg` prints the shots (cut times) and where the speaker's face is in each, and writes a gridded contact sheet. View the sheet. Shots with `"speaker": null` are B-roll. If the clip already has captions burned in, say so and ask for the clean file.
2. **Listen.** `python ~/kt/kt_words.py in.mp4 words.json en` (or `fa`, or no language for auto). Read the words against the footage and fix them: brand names are usually on a sign or logo in frame. Tell the user which words you were unsure of. Never invent speech.
3. **Design** (section 5), then write `edit.json` (section 4).
4. **Draft.** `python ~/kt/kt.py render in.mp4 edit.json draft.mp4 --to=12 --draft`, pull 10-12 stills with ffmpeg, tile them and look. Fix words hidden behind the head, text on the face, weak contrast, lines that got shrunk because they are too long, late or early words.
5. **Final.** `python ~/kt/kt.py render in.mp4 edit.json edit.mp4` (about 3-4 s of work per second of 720p video), then `python ~/kt/kt_audio.py edit.mp4 edit.json final.mp4`. Check stills from every shot and the loudness line.
6. **Deliver** final.mp4 with SendUserFile and a short Persian note: font and colours and why, which words were chosen as key words, words to confirm, what was not done.

## 4. edit.json

```json
{"style": {"font": "/path/Outfit.ttf", "font_rtl": "/path/Vazirmatn.ttf",
   "roles": {"key":   {"size": 236, "weight": 900, "color": "gold",  "track": -0.02},
             "mid":   {"size": 136, "weight": 700, "color": "white", "track": -0.02},
             "small": {"size": 90,  "weight": 400, "color": "white", "track": -0.01}},
   "colors": {"white": "#FFFFFF", "gold": "#FFE016", "red": "#FF2B1F", "grey": "#C9C9C9"},
   "leading": 0.2, "gap": 0.22, "safe": 0.9, "in": "pop", "hold": 1.4, "neg": 0.38, "shadow": [12, 150, 5]},
 "blocks": [
   {"tier": "top",   "lines": [[["You", 0.10, "small"]], [["know", 0.40, "key", {"blend": "negative", "in": "throw_l"}]], [["that", 0.62, "small"], ["agent", 0.84, "mid", {"color": "grey"}]]]},
   {"tier": "chest", "lines": [[["in", 1.16, "small"], ["your", 1.28, "small"]], [["market", 1.40, "key", {"color": "red", "in": "throw_l"}]]], "end": 2.4}],
 "zooms": [{"start": 0, "end": 4.9, "from": 1.0, "to": 1.06, "focus": [0.5, 0.3]}, {"start": 14.8, "end": 15.6, "type": "punch", "to": 1.1}],
 "whips": [{"t": 4.9}],
 "broll": [{"video": "/path/clip.mp4", "start": 6.0, "end": 8.0, "src_start": 0}],
 "inserts": [{"image": "/path/card.png", "start": 7.4, "end": 8.6, "at": [0.5, 0.3], "width": 0.6, "motion": "throw_t", "layer": "front"}],
 "sfx": [{"t": 12.2, "type": "riser", "mode": "end", "gain": -12}]}
```

- A word is `[text, time, role, {options}]`. Options: `color`, `blend` (`normal`, `negative`, `difference`, `multiply`, `overlay`, `screen`), `in`, `shine` (light sweep), `strike` (red line through it).
- `in` motions: `pop`, `slam`, `rise`, `drop`, `blur`, `wipe`, `throw_l`, `throw_r`, `throw_t`, `throw_b`, `none`. A block may set `in` for all its words and `out` (`throw_*`) to be thrown away at the end.
- `tier` `top` goes behind the speaker and is placed on the crown of the head; `chest` goes in front, on the chest. Both are placed automatically from the face found when the block starts. In a shot with no speaker nothing hides the text and the block falls back to a fixed position, so give B-roll blocks `"at": [x, y]` (0-1, centre of the stack) and `"layer": "front"`.
- Other block keys: `end` (otherwise the block lives until the next block of its tier, or `hold` seconds after its last word), `scale`, `align`, `overlap` (how far the top stack dips behind the head, in face heights), `follow` (track a walking speaker), `layer`.
- Sizes are pixels at 1080 wide and scale with the video. A stack wider than `safe` is shrunk; a top stack with no air above the head is shrunk to at most 68%.
- Gradient fill: `"fill": ["#top", "#middle", "#bottom"]` on a role or a word (any number of stops, top of the capitals to the descender).
- Attached word (kicker, superscript, label): a word with `"attach": {"to": "Global", "pos": "tl", "dx": 0, "dy": 0}`; `pos` is `tl`, `t`, `tr`, `bl`, `b`, `br`, `l`, `r`; `dx`/`dy` are in heights of the target word. It is left out of the line flow and drawn in front of the speaker (`"behind": true` to override). Write it in the same line as its target: `[["The", 0.0, "kick", {"attach": {"to": "Global", "pos": "tl"}}], ["Global", 0.24, "big"]]`.
- More motions: `reveal` (rises out of a slot at its own baseline, for numbers and hero words), `grow` (scale up with a small overshoot), `fade`.
- Pill or tag: `"box": {"stroke": 3, "stroke_color": "white", "pad": [0.9, 0.55]}` or `"box": {"color": "#86E53C", "alpha": 1}` on a role or word.
- Per-block `"leads": [0.42]` sets the gap before each following line (fraction of that line's height) when one block needs looser lines than the style.
- Cards: an insert with `"video": path, "src_start": s` (plays a clip inside the card) or `"image"`, plus `"size": [w, h]` (fractions of the frame width; cover-crops), `"radius": 0.1`, `"border": 2`, `"layer": "behind"` or `"front"`, `"motion": "grow"`, `"out": "pop"`, `"drift": [0, -14]` (px/s at 1080 wide), `"float": 10`, `"spin": 20` (deg/s). Cards get a soft shadow.
- Icon burst: `"bursts": [{"t": 15.9, "at": [0.31, 0.47], "images": [...], "spread": 0.2, "size": 0.12, "dur": 1.2, "stagger": 0.035, "spin": 25}]`. Icons must be the user's PNGs or ones you draw; no emoji font is installed.
- End card background: `"solids": [{"start": 32.1, "end": 35.7, "color": "#050505", "fade": 0.12, "glow": {"color": "#5FD12E", "at": [0.5, 0.96], "r": 0.55, "squash": 0.35, "strength": 0.55}}]`. A riser sound ends on it automatically.
- Logo watermark: an `"inserts"` image with `"width": 0.12, "at": [0.5, 0.05]`, running the whole video, `"silent": true`.
- **Style C keys.** `"in": "ghost"` (hero word lands half-transparent for 2 frames, then solid), `"in": "write"` (handwriting reveal in reading direction, right-to-left for Persian), `"in": "none"` for the small words. Per-block `"gap": 0.9` (wide word spacing) and `"leads": [-0.05, 0.1]` (small line touching the hero, tail line just under it). Word `"rot": -6` tilts it (for the handwritten accent). With `"align": "left"` or `"right"`, the x of `"at"` is that edge of the stack, and stacks are kept inside the frame. Persian lines flow right to left automatically (`"rtl": false` on a block to turn that off).

## 5. Design decisions (the part that matters most to this user)

**Type.** Choose the family from the tone of the speech and the brand, with a real weight range: geometric sans (Outfit, Poppins, Montserrat) for confident sales talk, wide display (Unbounded) for hype, serif display plus light sans (Playfair Display, DM Serif Display) for premium or calm, Vazirmatn or Lalezar for Persian. One family, three weights, big contrast. Say "closest free match", never "the same font".

**Colour.** Two highlight colours, white, one grey. Take them from the client's brand when it has one; otherwise pick against the footage. Check every key word on its actual frame.

**Key words.** Chosen by meaning: the promise, the product or name, numbers, contrasts, negations, the word the speaker leans on. About one per stack. Strike through the thing being denied.

**Splitting.** One breath is one stack of 2-5 words. Start the sentence in the top stack and land its last words on the chest. Up top, put small words above and the key word lowest so the head cuts into the big word, not into a small one. In close-ups (face taller than about 13% of the frame) keep the top stack to two lines and move the rest to the chest.

**Negative blend.** Use it on the coloured key words that sit behind the head, and on dark or mid-tone footage. Over white walls, sky or water it turns muddy: lower `neg`, or leave that word `normal`. Red and white words stay `normal`.

**Motion.** `pop` is the default. Throw one or two key words per sentence, alternating sides. `slam` only for the biggest number or claim. `shine` on a payoff word. Calm or premium clients: `rise`, `blur`, `wipe`, few throws.

**Style A or B.** A is louder: sales talk, offers, hot takes, real estate, coaching hooks. B is cleaner and more premium: events, launches, venues, corporate and tech, anything with a date, a place and a list of things. In B use one font family only (roman for hero words, italic for kickers, labels and captions; Inter Tight is the closest free match), two fills (silver and one brand colour), one hero word per beat, and put the cards on list sentences. Never copy the reference's logo, icons or event name; draw or ask for the client's own.

**Style C.** For podcasts, interviews, founders telling their story, LinkedIn, and anything reflective or personal. Take a neutral grotesque: Inter (closest free match to SF Pro, weight 700 hero, 600 small) or Inter Tight; for Persian, Vazirmatn or Estedad at the same weights (no lowercase rule, same spacing idea, right-aligned on the empty side). Handwriting: Caveat Brush for the yellow accent and Caveat for thin notes; for Persian, use a handwritten-looking face the user supplies, or skip the accent. Hero word = the one word the sentence leans on (a verb, a number, an emotion), one per phrase. The hero is about 23% of the width tall in 16:9, the small line about 6% of the width. Keep the speaker's empty side for text and let the hero dip behind the head only when it reads. Use the yellow accent at most once every 10-15 s, and the "about me" white page at most once per video. Every B-roll section uses the lower-third bar, never big type. Starting palette: white, #FFE600 yellow, black at 50% for the bar; for her own videos the accent can be gold instead of yellow.

**Style D.** For service promos, agencies, freelancers selling a service, coaches and real estate on a bright studio or white-wall background. Use when the speaker is filmed in one long wide take: the device transition, reframes and overlays replace cuts. Font: a tight neo-grotesque (Inter Tight or Manrope at 800 for heroes, 400 for kickers and karaoke lines). Palette: near-black text, one brand gradient for promise words, one warning gradient for the pain word, a platform gradient only when a platform is named. Keep the device transition to one per video and put it on the line that names the raw material or the before/after. CTA always ends on the typing bars with the client's own words. Ask for or draw the client's own UI details; never copy the reference's names, handles or brand logos (the camera and phone must be generic or the user's own photos).

**Style E.** For interview and podcast cutdowns with two people (host question -> guest answer), thought-leader takes, "the real question is" posts, and anything about a debate topic (AI, money, health). Needs a strong hook line in the first 3 s and a cliffhanger near the end; cut the source so the answer stops mid-sentence. Fonts: one clean sans for the small words, two or three display faces for hero words, each tied to a meaning (condensed = threat, round black = human, wide caps = action, serif = the turn). Colours: one danger colour, one positive colour, one payoff colour, all from the client brand when it has them; check pink and lime on both cameras. Put the name card on the first credential line, once. Use the decode reveal once per video, on the phrase that is the whole point. Persian: Vazirmatn for small words, Lalezar or Morabba for condensed hero words, Estedad Black for round, a Naskh face for the serif turn; right-aligned on the empty side. B-roll is metaphor, never literal stock; ask before generating.

**Camera and cuts.** A slow push-in (4-8%) on each talking shot, a `punch` on a number or the hook word, a whip on the big scene changes only (at most 3-4 per reel). B-roll needs real footage from the user; generating clips costs credits, so ask first.

**Sound.** kt_audio.py derives every effect from edit.json so each one lands on its move, and keeps them about 12 dB under the voice. Add a `riser` ending on a reveal with `"sfx"`. Music is separate: use the teaser-music-sfx skill if the user wants a bed.

    y0 = min(it["by"] for it in items); y1 = max(it["by"] + it["sp"]["cap"] for it in items)
    ox, oy = -(x0 + x1) / 2 if block.get("align", "center") == "center" else 0, -(y0 + y1) / 2
    if block.get("karaoke"):                                                 # Style D: whole line shown grey first, words turn dark as spoken
        for it in items: it["pre"] = typ.sprite(it["w"]["s"], it["w"]["role"], block["karaoke"], k, False, None, None)
    for it in items:
        it["bx"] += ox; it["by"] += oy; sp = it["sp"]
        it["x"] = it["bx"] - sp["pad"]; it["y"] = it["by"] + sp["cap"] - sp["base"]
    return {"items": items, "w": x1 - x0, "h": y1 - y0}

MOT_DUR = {"type": .5, "decode": 1.2, "glow": .34, "pop": .14, "slam": .17, "rise": .22, "drop": .22, "blur": .30, "wipe": .30, "fade": .18, "ghost": .13, "write": .35, "none": 0.001}
def motion(name, dt, u):
    """dt seconds since the word's time -> scale, dx, dy, alpha, blur, wipe"""
    if name.startswith("throw"):
        p = clamp(dt / 0.24); d = 1250 * u * (1 - back(p, 1.3))
        dx, dy = {"throw_l": (-d, 0), "throw_r": (d, 0), "throw_t": (0, -d), "throw_b": (0, d)}.get(name, (-d, 0))
        return 1, dx, dy, clamp(dt / 0.04), 0, 1
    p = ease(dt / MOT_DUR.get(name, .14)); q = 1 - p
    if name == "slam": return 1 + 0.9 * q * q, 0, 0, clamp(p * 3), 0, 1
    if name == "rise": return 1, 0, 80 * u * q, p, 0, 1
    if name == "drop": return 1, 0, -80 * u * q, p, 0, 1
    if name == "blur": return 1 + 0.10 * q, 0, 0, p, 18 * u * q, 1
    if name == "wipe": return 1, 0, 0, 1, 0, p
    if name == "write": return 1, 0, 0, 1, 0, clamp(dt / MOT_DUR["write"])      # handwriting: even left-to-right reveal
    if name in ("none", "type", "decode"): return 1, 0, 0, 1, 0, 1           # type / decode are masked letter by letter in render()
    if name == "glow": return 1 + 0.05 * q, 0, 0, clamp(p * 1.8), 16 * u * q, 1   # Style D: blurred word sharpens inside a fading halo
    if name == "fade": return 1, 0, 0, p, 0, 1
    if name == "ghost":                                                      # Style C hero word: lands half-transparent for ~2 frames, then solid
        return 1 + 0.03 * q, 0, 0, 0.45 if dt < 0.066 else clamp(0.45 + 0.55 * (dt - 0.066) / 0.06), 1.5 * u * q, 1
    if name == "grow": return 0.55 + 0.45 * back(dt / 0.22, 1.2), 0, 0, clamp(p * 2.5), 0, 1
    return 0.78 + 0.22 * back(dt / 0.16, 2.2), 0, 0, clamp(p * 2), 0, 1      # pop

def glyph_cols(alpha, text):
    """x ranges of the visible letters of a word sprite (left to right), for type / decode reveals"""
    n = sum(1 for c in text if not c.isspace())
    if n == 0: return []
    occ = alpha.max(0) > 24; runs = []; x = 0; Wd = len(occ)
    while x < Wd:
        if occ[x]:
            x0 = x
            while x < Wd and occ[x]: x += 1
            runs.append([x0, x])
        else: x += 1
    while len(runs) > n:                                                     # dots of i/j, accents: merge the narrowest gap
        g = min(range(len(runs) - 1), key=lambda i: runs[i + 1][0] - runs[i][1]); runs[g] = [runs[g][0], runs[g + 1][1]]; del runs[g + 1]
    while 0 < len(runs) < n:                                                 # touching letters: split the widest run
        g = max(range(len(runs)), key=lambda i: runs[i][1] - runs[i][0]); a, b = runs[g]; m = (a + b) // 2; runs[g:g + 1] = [[a, m], [m, b]]
    if runs:                                                                 # widen each range to the middle of the gaps so nothing is left behind
        for i in range(len(runs) - 1): m = (runs[i][1] + runs[i + 1][0]) // 2; runs[i][1] = m; runs[i + 1][0] = m
        runs[0][0] = 0; runs[-1][1] = Wd
    return runs

def letter_order(text, mode, seed=3, rtl=False):
    """order in which the letters of a word appear"""
    idx = [i for i, c in enumerate(text) if not c.isspace()]; k = list(range(len(idx)))
    if mode == "type": return k[::-1] if rtl else k
    first = [j for j, i in enumerate(idx) if i == 0 or text[i - 1].isspace()]
    rest = [j for j in k if j not in first]; random.Random(seed).shuffle(rest)
    return (first[::-1] if rtl else first) + rest

def mblur(arr, vx, vy):
    """directional blur of an RGBA array by a velocity in px/frame; returns (array, x offset, y offset)"""
    L = int(min(260, 0.75 * max(abs(vx), abs(vy))))
    if L < 4: return arr, 0, 0
    L |= 1; hz = abs(vx) >= abs(vy)
    arr = cv2.copyMakeBorder(arr, 0 if hz else L, 0 if hz else L, L if hz else 0, L if hz else 0, cv2.BORDER_CONSTANT, value=(0, 0, 0, 0))
    pm = arr.astype(np.float32); pm[..., :3] *= pm[..., 3:4] / 255                      # premultiply so colours do not darken
    pm = cv2.blur(pm, (L, 1) if hz else (1, L)); a = np.maximum(pm[..., 3:4], 1e-3)
    pm[..., :3] = pm[..., :3] / a * 255
    return np.clip(pm, 0, 255).astype(np.uint8), (-L if hz else 0), (0 if hz else -L)

class Canvas:
    def __init__(s, W, H): s.W, s.H = W, H; s.im = Image.new("RGBA", (W, H), (0, 0, 0, 0)); s.box = None
    def paste(s, sp, x, y):
        x, y = int(round(x)), int(round(y)); x0, y0 = max(0, x), max(0, y); x1, y1 = min(s.W, x + sp.width), min(s.H, y + sp.height)
        if x1 <= x0 or y1 <= y0: return
        s.im.alpha_composite(sp.crop((x0 - x, y0 - y, x1 - x, y1 - y)), (x0, y0))
        s.box = (x0, y0, x1, y1) if s.box is None else (min(s.box[0], x0), min(s.box[1], y0), max(s.box[2], x1), max(s.box[3], y1))

def composite(fr, cv, mode, matte, neg=0.5):
    """blend a canvas into the BGR frame inside its dirty box. matte hides it where the person is."""
    if cv.box is None: return
    x0, y0, x1, y1 = cv.box; o = np.asarray(cv.im.crop(cv.box)).astype(np.float32); al = o[..., 3:4] / 255
    if matte is not None: al = al * (1 - matte[y0:y1, x0:x1, None])
    bg = fr[y0:y1, x0:x1, ::-1].astype(np.float32); src = o[..., :3]
    if mode == "negative":                                                   # text brightness opposes the footage: bright on dark, muted on bright
        L = (bg @ np.array([0.299, 0.587, 0.114], np.float32))[..., None] / 255 * neg; mix = src * (1 - L) + bg * 0.35 * L
    elif mode == "difference": mix = np.abs(bg - src)
    elif mode == "multiply": mix = bg * src / 255
    elif mode == "screen": mix = 255 - (255 - bg) * (255 - src) / 255
    elif mode == "overlay":
        b, c = bg / 255, src / 255; mix = np.where(b < .5, 2 * b * c, 1 - 2 * (1 - b) * (1 - c)) * 255
    else: mix = src
    fr[y0:y1, x0:x1] = (bg * (1 - al) + mix * al).astype(np.uint8)[..., ::-1]

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ camera / transitions / b-roll
def zoom(fr, t, zooms, W, H):
    for z in zooms:
        if not (z["start"] <= t < z["end"]): continue
        u = (t - z["start"]) / (z["end"] - z["start"]); a, b = z.get("from", 1.0), z.get("to", 1.08)
        sh = ease(min(1, u / 0.18)) * (1 - ease(max(0, (u - 0.5) / 0.5))) if z.get("type") == "punch" else (u * u * (3 - 2 * u))
        sc = a + (b - a) * sh
        if abs(sc - 1) < 0.002: return fr
        fx, fy = z.get("focus", [0.5, 0.38]); M = cv2.getRotationMatrix2D((fx * W, fy * H), z.get("rot", 0) * sh, sc)
        return cv2.warpAffine(fr, M, (W, H), flags=cv2.INTER_LINEAR, borderMode=cv2.BORDER_REPLICATE)
    return fr

def whip(fr, t, whips, W, H, u):
    for w in whips:
        d = abs(t - w["t"]); half = w.get("dur", 0.3) / 2
        if d >= half: continue
        k = (1 - d / half) ** 1.4; L = int(w.get("strength", 110) * u * k) | 1
        if w.get("zoom", 0.05): fr = cv2.warpAffine(fr, cv2.getRotationMatrix2D((W / 2, H / 2), 0, 1 + w.get("zoom", 0.05) * k), (W, H), borderMode=cv2.BORDER_REPLICATE)
        if L >= 3: fr = cv2.blur(fr, (L, 1) if w.get("dir", "h") == "h" else (1, L), borderType=cv2.BORDER_REPLICATE)
        return fr
    return fr

def defocus(fr, t, items, W, H, u):
    """rack-focus out / in. {"start","end","mode": "out" | "in" | "both", "amount": 22, "zoom": 0.03}. The type on top stays sharp."""
    for d in items:
        if not (d["start"] <= t < d["end"]): continue
        p = (t - d["start"]) / max(1e-3, d["end"] - d["start"]); mode = d.get("mode", "out")
        k = ease(p) if mode == "out" else (1 - ease(p) if mode == "in" else np.sin(np.pi * p))
        if d.get("zoom", 0.03): fr = cv2.warpAffine(fr, cv2.getRotationMatrix2D((W / 2, H / 2), 0, 1 + d.get("zoom", 0.03) * k), (W, H), borderMode=cv2.BORDER_REPLICATE)
        sg = d.get("amount", 22) * u * k
        return cv2.GaussianBlur(fr, (0, 0), sg) if sg > 0.4 else fr
    return fr

def draw_strokes(cv, t, strokes, W, H, u, colors=None, fade_t=0.12):
    """hand-drawn lines and arrows that draw themselves: {"t","dur","end","paths":[[[x,y],...],...],"width","color"} (0-1 coords)"""
    for s in strokes:
        if not (s["t"] <= t < s.get("end", s["t"] + 2.0)): continue
        p = clamp((t - s["t"]) / s.get("dur", 0.35)); fa = clamp((s.get("end", s["t"] + 2.0) - t) / fade_t)
        paths = [[(x * W, y * H) for x, y in pa] for pa in s["paths"]]
        segs = [(a, b, np.hypot(b[0] - a[0], b[1] - a[1])) for pa in paths for a, b in zip(pa, pa[1:])]
        total = sum(L for *_, L in segs) or 1; left = p * total; xs = [c for pa in paths for c in pa]
        pad = 20 * u; x0, y0 = min(x for x, _ in xs) - pad, min(y for _, y in xs) - pad
        im = Image.new("RGBA", (int(max(x for x, _ in xs) - x0 + pad), int(max(y for _, y in xs) - y0 + pad)), (0, 0, 0, 0)); d = ImageDraw.Draw(im)
        c_ = s.get("color", "#111111"); col = hexrgb((colors or {}).get(c_, c_)) + (int(255 * fa),); wd = max(2, int(s.get("width", 5) * u))
        for a, b, L in segs:
            if left <= 0: break
            f = min(1, left / max(L, 1e-6)); b2 = (a[0] + (b[0] - a[0]) * f, a[1] + (b[1] - a[1]) * f)
            d.line([(a[0] - x0, a[1] - y0), (b2[0] - x0, b2[1] - y0)], fill=col, width=wd)
            for c in (a, b2): d.ellipse([c[0] - x0 - wd / 2, c[1] - y0 - wd / 2, c[0] - x0 + wd / 2, c[1] - y0 + wd / 2], fill=col)   # round caps and joins
            left -= L
        cv.paste(im, x0, y0)

class Broll:
    def __init__(s, items): s.items = items; s.caps = {}; s.last = {}
    def __call__(s, fr, t, W, H):
        for k, b in enumerate(s.items):
            if not (b["start"] <= t < b["end"]): continue
            if k not in s.caps:
                s.caps[k] = cv2.VideoCapture(b["video"]); s.caps[k].set(cv2.CAP_PROP_POS_MSEC, b.get("src_start", 0) * 1000)
            ok, f = s.caps[k].read()
            if ok: s.last[k] = f
            f = s.last.get(k)
            if f is None: return fr
            sc = max(W / f.shape[1], H / f.shape[0]); f = cv2.resize(f, (int(f.shape[1] * sc) + 1, int(f.shape[0] * sc) + 1))
            y0, x0 = (f.shape[0] - H) // 2, (f.shape[1] - W) // 2; f = f[y0:y0 + H, x0:x0 + W]
            if b.get("bw"):                                                       # black-and-white grade with a little extra contrast
                g = cv2.cvtColor(f, cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY).astype(np.float32); g = np.clip((g - 128) * b.get("contrast", 1.12) + 128, 0, 255).astype(np.uint8)
                f = cv2.cvtColor(g, cv2.COLOR_GRAY2BGR)
            fd = b.get("fade", 0.0); a = 1.0 if fd <= 0 else clamp(min((t - b["start"]) / fd, (b["end"] - t) / fd))
            return cv2.addWeighted(f, a, fr, 1 - a, 0) if a < 1 else f
        return fr

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ render
def prepare(edit, typ, W, H):
    blocks = []
    for b in edit.get("blocks", []):
        b = dict(b); b["_lines"] = [[word_of(w) for w in ln] for ln in b["lines"]]
        b.setdefault("tier", "top"); b.setdefault("layer", "behind" if b["tier"] == "top" else "front")
        ws = [w for ln in b["_lines"] for w in ln]; b["_t0"] = b.get("start", min(w["t"] for w in ws)); b["_last"] = max(w["t"] for w in ws)
        blocks.append(b)
    blocks.sort(key=lambda b: b["_t0"])
    for i, b in enumerate(blocks):                                           # a block lives until the next one in its tier, or a short hold
        nxt = next((c["_t0"] for c in blocks[i + 1:] if c["tier"] == b["tier"]), 1e9)
        b["_t1"] = b.get("end", min(nxt - 0.02, b["_last"] + edit["style"].get("hold", 1.4)))
        b["_lay"] = layout(b, typ, W, H)
    ins_all = list(edit.get("inserts", []))
    for bu in edit.get("bursts", []):                                         # icons that fly out of one point
        n = len(bu["images"]); r0 = bu.get("spread", 0.22); a0 = bu.get("angle", -90)
        for j, img in enumerate(bu["images"]):
            ang = np.radians(a0 + 360 * j / n + bu.get("twist", 0)); rr = r0 * (0.75 + 0.25 * ((j * 7) % 3) / 2)
            ins_all.append({"image": img, "start": bu["t"] + j * bu.get("stagger", 0.035), "end": bu["t"] + bu.get("dur", 1.6), "at": bu["at"],
                            "width": bu.get("size", 0.11), "fly": [rr * np.cos(ang), rr * np.sin(ang) * W / H], "motion": "grow",
                            "spin": bu.get("spin", 25) * (1 if j % 2 else -1), "layer": bu.get("layer", "front"), "out": "pop", "_burst": True})
    edit["_inserts"] = ins_all
    for ins in ins_all:
        if "video" in ins:
            ins["_cap"] = cv2.VideoCapture(ins["video"]); ins["_cap"].set(cv2.CAP_PROP_POS_MSEC, ins.get("src_start", 0) * 1000); ins["_im"] = None
        else: ins["_im"] = card(Image.open(ins["image"]).convert("RGBA"), ins, W)
    return blocks

def card(im, ins, W):
    """size the image for an insert. "size":[w,h] (fractions of width) cover-crops into a card; "radius" rounds the corners;
    "border" draws an outline; otherwise the image keeps its aspect at "width"."""
    if "size" in ins:
        cw, ch = int(ins["size"][0] * W), int(ins["size"][1] * W); sc = max(cw / im.width, ch / im.height)
        im = im.resize((max(1, int(im.width * sc + .5)), max(1, int(im.height * sc + .5))), Image.LANCZOS)
        l, t = (im.width - cw) // 2, (im.height - ch) // 2; im = im.crop((l, t, l + cw, t + ch))
    else:
        k = ins.get("width", 0.5) * W / im.width; im = im.resize((max(1, int(im.width * k)), max(1, int(im.height * k))), Image.LANCZOS)
    if ins.get("radius"):
        r = int(ins["radius"] * min(im.size)); m = Image.new("L", im.size, 0); ImageDraw.Draw(m).rounded_rectangle([0, 0, im.width - 1, im.height - 1], r, fill=255)
        a = np.minimum(np.asarray(im.getchannel("A")), np.asarray(m)); im.putalpha(Image.fromarray(a))
        if ins.get("border"): ImageDraw.Draw(im).rounded_rectangle([0, 0, im.width - 1, im.height - 1], r, outline=hexrgb(ins.get("border_color", "#FFFFFF")) + (200,), width=max(1, int(ins["border"] * W / 1080)))
    if ins.get("shadow", True) and "size" in ins:
        p = int(0.06 * W); sh = Image.new("RGBA", (im.width + 2 * p, im.height + 2 * p), (0, 0, 0, 0))
        sm = Image.new("RGBA", sh.size, (0, 0, 0, 0)); sm.paste((0, 0, 0, 120), (p, int(p * 1.25), p + im.width, int(p * 1.25) + im.height), im.getchannel("A"))
        sh.alpha_composite(sm.filter(ImageFilter.GaussianBlur(p / 3))); sh.alpha_composite(im, (p, p)); im = sh
    if ins.get("rotate"): im = im.rotate(ins["rotate"], expand=True, resample=Image.BICUBIC)
    return im

def anchor_for(b, box, W, H):
    """centre of a block. 'at' wins; otherwise placed from the speaker's face: top tier sits on the crown of the head
    (so the head overlaps its last line), chest tier sits on the chest."""
    lay = b["_lay"]
    if "at" in b:                                                            # with align left / right, the x of "at" is that edge of the stack
        sh = {"left": lay["w"] / 2, "right": -lay["w"] / 2}.get(b.get("align"), 0)
        x = b["at"][0] * W + sh; x = x if lay["w"] > 0.94 * W else clamp(x, lay["w"] / 2 + 0.03 * W, W - lay["w"] / 2 - 0.03 * W)   # never off the edge
        return x, b["at"][1] * H
    if box is None: return 0.5 * W, (0.28 if b["tier"] == "top" else 0.62) * H
    cx = clamp(box["cx"] * W, lay["w"] / 2 + 0.04 * W, W - lay["w"] / 2 - 0.04 * W)
    if b["tier"] == "top":
        bottom = (box["top"] + b.get("overlap", 0.0 if box["face_h"] > 0.13 else 0.35) * box["face_h"]) * H; cy = max(bottom - lay["h"] / 2, 0.07 * H + lay["h"] / 2)
    else:
        cy = (box["chin"] + 1.0 * box["face_h"]) * H + lay["h"] * 0.15; cy = min(cy, 0.82 * H - lay["h"] / 2)
    return cx, cy

def render(src, edit, out, t_from=0.0, t_to=None, draft=False):
    cap = cv2.VideoCapture(src); W, H = int(cap.get(3)), int(cap.get(4)); FPS = cap.get(5) or 30; N = int(cap.get(7))
    st = edit["style"]; u = W / 1080.0; typ = Type(st, W); blocks = prepare(edit, typ, W, H)
    broll = Broll(edit.get("broll", [])); matte = None; face = Face(W, H); every = 6 if draft else st.get("matte_every", 2)
    n0 = int(t_from * FPS); n1 = N if t_to is None else min(N, int(t_to * FPS)); cap.set(cv2.CAP_PROP_POS_FRAMES, n0)
    ff = subprocess.Popen(["ffmpeg", "-y", "-v", "error", "-f", "rawvideo", "-pix_fmt", "bgr24", "-s", f"{W}x{H}", "-r", f"{FPS}", "-i", "-",
                           "-ss", f"{n0 / FPS}", "-i", src, "-map", "0:v", "-map", "1:a?", "-c:v", "libx264", "-crf", "23" if draft else "17",
                           "-preset", "veryfast", "-pix_fmt", "yuv420p", "-c:a", "aac", "-b:a", "256k", "-shortest", "-movflags", "+faststart", out], stdin=subprocess.PIPE)
    m = None; neg = st.get("neg", 0.5)
    for n in range(n0, n1):
        ok, fr = cap.read()
        if not ok: break
        t = n / FPS; fr = zoom(broll(fr, t, W, H), t, edit.get("zooms", []), W, H)
        for so in edit.get("solids", []):
            if so["start"] <= t < so["end"]:
                a_ = clamp(min((t - so["start"]) / so.get("fade", 0.2), (so["end"] - t) / so.get("fade", 0.2)) if so.get("fade", 0.2) > 0 else 1)
                bgc = np.zeros_like(fr); bgc[:] = hexrgb(so.get("color", "#000000"))[::-1]
                if so.get("glow"):
                    gl = so["glow"]; yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:H, 0:W]; gx, gy = gl.get("at", [0.5, 0.9]); rr = gl.get("r", 0.35) * W
                    e = np.exp(-(((xx - gx * W) / rr) ** 2 + ((yy - gy * H) / (rr * gl.get("squash", 0.6))) ** 2))[..., None] * gl.get("strength", 0.8)
                    bgc = (bgc * (1 - e) + np.array(hexrgb(gl["color"])[::-1], np.float32) * e).astype(np.uint8)
                fr = cv2.addWeighted(bgc, a_, fr, 1 - a_, 0) if a_ < 1 else bgc
        fr = defocus(fr, t, edit.get("defocus", []), W, H, u)
        act = [b for b in blocks if b["_t0"] - 0.02 <= t < b["_t1"]]
        ins = [i for i in edit["_inserts"] if i["start"] <= t < i["end"]]
        nop = any(a <= t < b for a, b in edit.get("no_person", [])); box = None
        if any(("_at" not in b and "at" not in b) or "_p" not in b or b.get("follow") for b in act):
            box = None if nop else face(fr)
        for b in act:
            if "_p" not in b: b["_p"] = box is not None                          # is the speaker in this shot? if not, nothing can hide the text
        need = any(b["layer"] == "behind" and b["_p"] for b in act) or any(i.get("layer") == "behind" for i in ins)
        if need and not nop:
            if matte is None: matte = Matte()
            if m is None or (n - n0) % every == 0: m = matte(fr)
        else: m = None; matte and setattr(matte, "prev", None)
        cvs = {}
        def cv_of(layer, mode):
            if (layer, mode) not in cvs: cvs[(layer, mode)] = Canvas(W, H)
            return cvs[(layer, mode)]
        for i in ins:                                                            # cards, icons, logos
            if "_cap" in i:
                ok_, f_ = i["_cap"].read()
                if ok_: i["_im"] = card(Image.fromarray(f_[..., ::-1]).convert("RGBA"), i, W)
                if i["_im"] is None: continue
            dt = t - i["start"]; mo = i.get("motion", "pop"); sc, dx, dy, al, bl, wp = motion(mo, dt, u); sp = i["_im"]
            _, dx2, dy2, *_ = motion(mo, dt - 1 / FPS, u); fo = clamp((i["end"] - t) / 0.14)
            if i.get("out") == "pop": sc *= 0.6 + 0.4 * fo

def _fade(x, a=0.004, b=0.012):
